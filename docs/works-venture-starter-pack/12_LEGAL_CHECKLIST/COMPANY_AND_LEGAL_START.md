# Company & Legal Start Checklist

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice.

- Select entity/jurisdiction with lawyer/accountant.
- Founder/IP assignment.
- Cap table.
- Contractor/employee IP terms.
- Trademark and domain clearance for final name.
- Terms of Service.
- Privacy Policy.
- DPA.
- Subprocessor list.
- Security/vulnerability disclosure policy.
- Pilot/design-partner agreement.
- SLA/support terms before enterprise GA.
- Open-source license policy.
- Data retention/deletion policy.
- Insurance assessment.
- VAT/tax/billing setup.
- Export/sanctions review if relevant to infrastructure customers.
