# Business Model

## Revenue
- SaaS subscription
- hosted compute margin
- BYOC control-plane fee
- enterprise governance/security
- dedicated/private control plane
- premium support/SLA

## Pricing hypothesis
Developer: free limited tier  
Pro: ~$20/user/month + compute  
Team: ~$40/user/month + compute  
Enterprise: custom

Pricing is provisional until pilots establish willingness-to-pay and value attribution.
