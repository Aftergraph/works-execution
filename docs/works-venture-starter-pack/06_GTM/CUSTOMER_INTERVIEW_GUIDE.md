# Customer Interview Guide

1. Walk me through the last CI failure that wasted meaningful time.
2. What is P50/P95 time-to-green?
3. What portion is queue, setup, build, test and deploy?
4. What do you spend monthly on CI and related runner infrastructure?
5. What workloads cannot use hosted runners?
6. How do you manage caches?
7. How often are jobs retried because of infrastructure/flakiness?
8. What would make you switch runner providers?
9. What would prevent you from moving execution off GitHub-hosted runners?
10. How are coding agents changing CI volume?
11. Who owns the budget?
12. What proof would justify a paid pilot?
