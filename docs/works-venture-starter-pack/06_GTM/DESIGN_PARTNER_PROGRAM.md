# Design Partner Program

## Target
3-5 teams with real CI pain and willingness to share operational metrics.

## Required baseline data
- pipeline duration P50/P95
- queue wait
- monthly CI spend
- runner shapes
- failure/retry rate
- manual interventions
- repository size/build system
- security constraints

## Pilot success
At least one:
- >=30% lower time-to-green
- >=30% lower execution cost
- material reduction in manual CI intervention

With no reduction in required verification confidence.
